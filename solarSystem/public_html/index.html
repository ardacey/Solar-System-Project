Solar System Controls

  • H - Toggle Help Menu
  • P - Activate Camera and Control Mode
  • K - See Hall of Fame
  • M - Manual Movement for Earth
  • Escape - Deactivate Camera and Control Mode
  • LeftMouse Drag - Rotate the Camera
  • RightMouse Drag - Move the Camera
  • Mouse Wheel - Zoom In/Out
  • Buttons - Center the view on the selected celestial body or spaceship
  • W - Move the spaceship forward
  • A - Move the spaceship left
  • S - Move the spaceship backward
  • D - Move the spaceship right
